# Neon Noir

A neon-noir theme for Claude Desktop: a megacity after midnight, in hazard yellow,
electric cyan and neon red.

Install with the rest of Clarity (`npx github:swperb/clarity`), or on its own in
Claude Desktop: click **+** next to the prompt box → **Plugins** → **Add marketplace**
`swperb/clarity` → add **neon-noir**.

## What you get

- **A city behind the chat.** Three layers of towers with lit windows and blinking
  beacons, neon tubes on the near towers, and two angular holographic billboards (cut
  corners, hazard stripes) whose emblems drift apart into red and cyan now and then.
- **Slow motion.** Thin data streams fall down both sides of the window, traffic
  crosses the sky, searchlights sweep, a red grid floor drifts toward you, rain comes
  down over it all, and very rarely the picture tears.
- **A faint CRT finish.** Scanlines, a slow rolling band of light and a vignette.
- **Neon in the interface.** Hazard yellow for focus, links and keywords; electric cyan
  for highlights and code literals; danger a neon red, success an acid green, warning a
  burnt orange. Surfaces are blue-steel black, a few percent apart.
- **No text in the scene**, so nothing competes with the conversation.

## Still readable

- The app frosts its panels over the scene, and the city keeps its tallest towers,
  billboards and data streams at the sides, with a darker pool in the middle.
- Primary text is 7:1+ contrast on every surface; muted text clears 4.5:1.
- Diffs keep the app's standard green and red: Neon Noir never sets git colors.
- With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, the whole scene holds still.

Dark only: this city has no daytime. The pointer stays the app's own (a theme can't
change it).

## With the rest of Clarity

Neon Noir counts as "another theme" to Clarity's plugins: the Clarity theme steps aside
for it, and Factory's project tint changes only its accent, keeping its surfaces and
city. `/factory tint off` brings Neon Noir back exactly.
